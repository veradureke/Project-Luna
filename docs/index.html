Mission Overview

A field guide to the Moon, worn as a headset

Project LUNA turns the Artemis program into something visitors can walk through — built for classrooms, open houses, and public exhibits at NASA Glenn.

01 · CONTEXT

Why it exists

Artemis is complex — lunar landers, ISRU, gateway stations. LUNA gives visitors a guide instead of a wall of text, letting them ask questions in their own words.

02 · APPROACH

How it teaches

Three linked demos — inspect real NASA hardware models, ask LUNA anything by voice, or follow a waypoint tour of the mission timeline.

03 · WHERE IT RUNS

Built for the floor

Standalone on Meta Quest 3, tethered to a PC server for AI inference — designed to survive a full day of public demo traffic.

Meet the Guide

LUNA — the astronaut who never leaves the exhibit

LUNA is a world-space AI companion who follows visitors through the experience, answering questions about the Moon, Artemis hardware, and NASA's return-to-lunar-surface plans in natural conversation — no menus, no scripts to memorize.

Speech goes in through Whisper, gets routed through a NASA-grounded knowledge base, and comes back out as LUNA's voice — all running on a tethered PC server so the headset stays light and untethered for the visitor.

The Experience

Three ways to explore the mission

01

AI Object Inspector

Pick up real NASA hardware models — landers, rovers, suits — and ask LUNA what you're holding and why it matters.

02

Voice Q&A

Talk to LUNA directly. Questions are transcribed, grounded against NASA mission data, and answered out loud in real time.

03

Waypoint Tour Guide

Follow a guided path through a lunar terrain scene, with LUNA narrating each stop along the Artemis timeline.

System Architecture

How a voice becomes an answer

Everything below runs across a Quest 3 headset and a tethered PC server, rebuilt this cycle for stability under real exhibit conditions.

Input

Mic capture

Quest 3 microphone streams audio to the tethered server.

→
Transcribe

Whisper STT

Speech converted to text locally on the PC server.

→
Ground

NASA Luna proxy

Query routed through a NASA-grounded knowledge base for accurate answers.

→
Voice

pyttsx3 TTS

Response synthesized to speech and streamed back to the headset.

→
Output

LUNA speaks

World-space companion animates and delivers the answer in-scene.
